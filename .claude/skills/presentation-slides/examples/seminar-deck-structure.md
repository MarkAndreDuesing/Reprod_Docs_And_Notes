# Example configuration: two-paper seminar discussion deck

One deck structure used with this skill (LMU seminar, ~90 minutes, two papers). Use it as a
starting point when the user asks for a full discussion deck and gives no other structure.

1. Title: "Lecture N: Paper Discussion"; the papers (authors, venue, year); seminar and date.
2. Index: the parts below, numbered.
3. Paper 1: section slide (subtitle = full reference), then General Summary, Terminology /
   Definitions, Research Questions, Methodology, Results, Validity / Discussion.
4. Paper 1 · Discussion: question slides.
5. Paper 2: same as Paper 1.
6. Paper 2 · Discussion: question slides.
7. Cross Comparison: section slide, a differences table, a similarities slide.
8. Discussion: What Do You Agree With?: question slides on the comparison.
9. Criticism: section slide, one slide per paper.
10. Open Floor: audience points prepared in advance.
11. References (if any [n] citations), then "Thank you for your attention".

The worked-example scripts in this folder are from that deck (Vorl_2). They are paper-specific;
use `build_slides.py` with a content file for new work.
