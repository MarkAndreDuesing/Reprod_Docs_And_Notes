## Discussion questions

1. **Same or different picture?** Do the results agree despite different
   fields, times or methods?
2. **Competing diagnoses / solutions.** Which convinces you more, and why?
3. **What neither paper measures.** What would a study need to look like to
   close that gap?
4. **Direct disagreements** between the papers (e.g. recommended tools or practices).
5. **Shared concepts under different names**, and how the lecture names them.

## Broader / subjective discussion

- What does the central concept mean to *you*?
- How do incentives (novelty vs. confirmation) shape the problem?
- What is one thing you would do differently in your own work after reading these?
- Who should act first: researchers, supervisors, venues, funders, universities?
