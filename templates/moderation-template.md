# Moderation Plan (Template)

For weeks where the user **moderates** the seminar discussion. Save as
`Vorl_N/moderation.md`. Builds on the discussion files, but is written to be
used live: a short question sequence, not an analysis.

## What makes a good discussion question

From the user's notes after week 1:

- **Leads to real discussion:** not too obvious (everyone agrees in one
  sentence), but not so open that people shrug and say "I don't know".
- **Has a foothold in the paper:** people can argue from something concrete
  (a number, a proposal, a definition), plus their own experience.
- **Some outside-the-box questions:** reframe the topic, take a different
  perspective (another stakeholder, another field, a point far in the future),
  or question an assumption both papers share.
- **Solutions over blame:** ask what would fix a problem rather than which
  party is at fault.

Quick test for each question: can you name two reasonable, opposing answers?
If not, rework it.

- One-sentence summary of each paper and the shared theme.

### 2. Warm-up question
- One concrete, easy-to-enter question tied to a striking finding.

### 4. Outside-the-box questions (1–2)
- Example of the kind of question meant (from week 1): "If artifacts were

### 5. Wrap-up
- What would we change, based on the papers?

## Background to have ready
Short facts that help answer side questions (gather with web search, mark
anything recalled from memory):
- current state of the practices the papers discuss, vs. when they were written
- basics of any technology the papers rely on
- differences between research and industry where the topic touches both
